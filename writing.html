Writer Bio

Daniel Jenkins is a screenwriter drawn to engaging, high-energy action/adventure stories with a sense of wonder. His work often centers on friendship, understanding, and redemption, usually following roguish protagonists who get themselves into situations way bigger than they expected.

He is currently developing The Time Traveler's Journal, a time-travel heist about a treasure hunter attempting to steal the Amber Room before it disappears from history.

Outside of writing, Daniel is a film student and game developer who also fixes and sells old ThinkPads on the side.
